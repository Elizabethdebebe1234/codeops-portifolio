Exercise: Inspect an Ethiopian website

Website inspected: Ethiopian Airlines

What I found:

<header>
The top section of the website.
It contains the Ethiopian Airlines logo, search options, and booking information.
<nav>
The navigation bar below or within the header.
It contains links such as Book, Manage Booking, Experience, Travel Information, and Help.
<footer>
The bottom section of the page.
It contains copyright information, contact details, social media links, and other useful links.
